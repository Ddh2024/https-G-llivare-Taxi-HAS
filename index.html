    Good to know

    How do I know the price?

    Send a booking request and we reply with the price before the ride. Every taxi also shows its comparison price (jämförpris) on the window, as Swedish law requires.

    My flight is delayed — what happens?

    Add your flight number when you book. We follow arrivals and adjust the pick-up time.

    Can you take skis, child seats or a big group?

    Yes. Tick the options in the booking form. Our minivan seats up to 8 passengers with luggage.

    Do you drive outside Kiruna?

    Yes — Jukkasjärvi, Abisko, Björkliden, Riksgränsen, Nikkaluokta, Gällivare, Narvik and anywhere else in the region. Ask for a fixed price for long trips.

    Can my company pay by invoice?

    Yes. Tick "Company invoice" in the booking form or e-mail us to set up an agreement.

    What happens to my personal data?

    This website does not use cookies or tracking and does not store your booking. Your request goes directly to us via WhatsApp or e-mail and is used only to carry out your trip.
